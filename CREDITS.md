## Educational References

The path tracing implementation is based on principles described in the following references:

- **Peter Shirley** — *Ray Tracing in One Weekend*, *Ray Tracing: The Next Week*, and *Ray Tracing: The Rest of Your Life* book series. Available at [raytracing.github.io](https://raytracing.github.io/).
- **Matt Pharr, Wenzel Jakob, and Greg Humphreys** — *Physically Based Rendering: From Theory to Implementation* (PBRT). Available at [pbr-book.org](https://www.pbr-book.org/).
- **Krzysztof Narkowicz** — ACES filmic tonemapping curve fit. Reference: [knarkowicz.wordpress.com](https://knarkowicz.wordpress.com/2016/01/06/aces-filmic-tone-mapping-curve/).
- **Tomas Möller and Ben Trumbore** — *Fast, Minimum Storage Ray/Triangle Intersection*, 1997.
- **Ingo Wald et al.** — *On fast Construction of SAH-based Bounding Volume Hierarchies*, 2007. Used for BVH construction strategy.
